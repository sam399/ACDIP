Frequently Asked Questions & Safety Guidelines

The HVI is a weighted index calculated dynamically from demographic vulnerability inputs. It weighs factors such as pregnant women (+20), disabled members (+20), elderly members (+15), chronic illnesses (+15), and young children (+12). These inputs allow the dispatch pipeline to automatically identify and rank high-risk households in the AI Priority Queue.

Every citizen emergency SOS report and infrastructure damage report receives a credibility score (0%–100%). It grants +30% for uploaded photo evidence, +30% if multiple similar incidents occur within a 5km radius, +20% if the description contains detailed text (>50 chars), and has a 20% baseline trust value.
